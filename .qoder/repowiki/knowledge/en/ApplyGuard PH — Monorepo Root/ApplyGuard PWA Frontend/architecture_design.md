Entry `src/main.jsx` mounts React under `<BrowserRouter>` and registers `/sw.js` as the PWA service worker; `src/App.jsx` is the route table that nests every page component inside `<AuthProvider>` (from `auth.jsx`) and `<AppProvider>` (from `store.jsx`), all wrapped by the shared `<Layout />` shell. Pages are split across child modules — free scanner (`ScanForm`, `ResultView`, `Tracker`) in `interview_tracker`, premium flows (`OffersPage`, `AccountPage`, `MockInterviewPage`) in `interview_pages` and `account_settings` — but they share the same two context providers and the Tailwind-based layout. The `lib/` directory holds domain-agnostic utilities (supabase client, billing, entitlement, scoring, CSV, followups, redflags, sync) consumed uniformly by both free and premium pages, while `public/` supplies the PWA manifest, OG assets, and the Capacitor-compatible `sw.js`. Mobile builds are gated through `mobile.js` which wraps the same entry when Capacitor is present.
